About me

Careful assessment, evidence-based treatment, and time for the person.

I'm a physiotherapist from Chennai, now in Bengaluru for my Master of Physiotherapy in Neurology at Yenepoya (Deemed to be University).

My six-month rotatory internship across three Chennai hospitals gave me hands-on experience in every major specialty, from cardiopulmonary care to sports rehab. It built the habit of assessing carefully, treating with evidence, and never losing sight of the person behind the diagnosis.

I'm a published researcher and an award-winning presenter, and I aim to be a calm, reassuring presence for patients and their families.
